Supplementary Figure 15. Comparison of the immune cell subpopulations detected by spectral flow cytometry and scRNA-seq. a Stacked bar plots showing the proportions of major cell types identified by spectral flow cytometry (SFC) in individual samples from the PBMC, liver, thymus, and spleen. Samples are ordered by pcw and the tissue origin. The frequencies of cell types in PBMC are presented as percentages of total lymphocytes while the frequencies in the liver, thymus, and spleen are based on total CD45+ cells. b Stacked bar plots showing the proportions of the major cell types identified by scRNA-seq in the individual samples. For the scRNA-seq analysis, quantification was performed on CD45+ cells, following the exclusion of erythrocytes and megakaryocytes. Eleven major cell types were used for the comparison with the populations identified by SFC.
